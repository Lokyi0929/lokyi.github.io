About Our University

Our Mission

YaoYang University is committed to delivering high-quality education and nurturing the intellectual and personal growth of its students. The college aims to produce graduates who are well-prepared to meet the challenges of their respective fields and to contribute positively to society.

We strive to empower our students with the knowledge, skills, and ethical foundations necessary to thrive in a complex and rapidly changing world, encouraging them to become lifelong learners and responsible global citizens.

Our History

Founded in 1985, YaoYang University began as a small college dedicated to technological advancements. Over the decades, it has grown exponentially, expanding its academic offerings and global reach. Today, we stand as a beacon of academic excellence, known for our rigorous programs, groundbreaking research, and a commitment to nurturing talent.

Our journey has been marked by significant milestones, including the establishment of new faculties, pioneering research breakthroughs, and the continuous development of state-of-the-art facilities, all contributing to our reputation as a leading educational institution.
