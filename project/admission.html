Admission Requirements

Academic Requirements

  • Minimum 4 creadit in SPM.
  • Mathematic must have creadit in SPM.
  • SPM Graduation Certificate.
  • English needs to be above grade B in SPM.

Required Documents

  • Completed online application form.
  • Official academic transcripts.
  • Curriculum Vitae (CV) or Resume.
  • Copy of passport/ID.
